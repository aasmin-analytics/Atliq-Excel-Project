# AtliQ Hardware – Excel Business Analysis

## Project Overview

This project is an **Excel-based business analysis of AtliQ Hardware**, focused on analyzing sales performance, market performance, product performance, and P&L metrics for FY 2019, FY 2020, and FY 2021.

The analysis provides a view of business performance across different customers, markets, products, and months.

## Project Objectives

* Analyze customer-wise sales performance
* Compare market-wise performance against targets
* Analyze product-wise sales growth
* Evaluate monthly and quarterly P&L performance
* Compare business performance across financial years
* Identify changes in Net Sales, COGS, Gross Margin, and GM%

## Analysis Covered

### 1. Customer Performance

Analyzed Net Sales performance of customers in the Indian market and compared sales across FY 2019, FY 2020, and FY 2021.

### 2. Market Performance

Compared market-wise Net Sales with the 2021 target and analyzed the variance from target.

### 3. Product Performance

Analyzed product-wise sales for FY 2020 and FY 2021 and calculated the percentage change between the two years.

### 4. P&L Analysis

Analyzed:

* Net Sales
* COGS
* Gross Margin
* Gross Margin %
* Monthly and quarterly performance

The P&L analysis covers FY 2019, FY 2020, and FY 2021.
